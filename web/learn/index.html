Microphone off. Speak to navigate or answer.

What can I say?

Say “Explore”, “Practice”, “Story mode”, “About me”, “Home”, “Back”, “Help”, “Repeat”, “Stop speech”, or “Stop listening”. You can also say “turn on spoken navigation”. In Practice, say a letter or answer a mystery. In a book, say “next page”, “read story”, “pause reading”, or “continue story”. Reading pauses command recognition to prevent echoes; use Pause reading or Stop speech to give a command. In Explore, say “start camera” and “choose” an object.

While listening, microphone audio is sent to ElevenLabs for transcription. Stop listening closes the microphone.
