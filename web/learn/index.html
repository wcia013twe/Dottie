Voice settings

Microphone off

Microphone off. Speak to navigate or answer.

What can I say?

Speak naturally: “I want to practice”, “let’s read a story”, “can you show me my progress?”, “take me back home”, or “can you help me?”. Short commands such as “Explore”, “Back”, “Repeat”, “Stop speech”, and “Stop listening” still work. Everyday wording works too, such as “let’s go explore” or “I’d love to settle down with a book”. If a request is unclear, we’ll ask you to choose. You can also say “turn on spoken navigation”. In Practice, say a letter or answer a mystery. In a book, say “next page”, “read story”, “pause reading”, or “continue story”. Reading pauses command recognition to prevent echoes; use Pause reading or Stop speech to give a command. In Explore, say “start camera” and “choose” an object.

While listening, microphone audio is sent to ElevenLabs for transcription. Unrecognized navigation requests and the current page name go to the existing Databricks model for interpretation; story answers stay in the story workflow. Stop listening closes the microphone.

A little guidance, whenever you need it.
